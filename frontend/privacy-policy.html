2. What data the Site collects

2a. Webcam video

When you click "Start Stream", your browser requests access to your webcam. Webcam video never leaves your browser. Hand-landmark detection runs entirely in-browser using WebAssembly (MediaPipe Tasks Vision). No video frames, images, or landmarks are transmitted to any server operated by this Site.

2b. Gesture templates (localStorage)

When you record a gesture, the resulting feature data is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key slt_gestures. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the Site operator. You can delete it at any time through your browser's developer tools or by clearing site data.

2c. Third-party CDN connections

Loading the Site causes your browser to make connections to the following third-party services. These services may log your IP address and other standard HTTP request headers in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — used to load the DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono typefaces. Google Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — used to load the MediaPipe Tasks Vision JavaScript bundle. jsDelivr Privacy Policy.
  • Google Cloud Storage (storage.googleapis.com) — used to download the hand-landmarker machine-learning model file. Google Privacy Policy.

The Site operator does not receive or store any data from these connections. These connections are technically necessary for the Site to function.

2d. Optional backend WebSocket

The Site may establish a WebSocket connection to a companion server hosted on Render.com (sign-language-translator-with-custom.onrender.com) for optional server-side processing. If this connection is made, your IP address is visible to the Render.com server. No personal data is intentionally collected or logged by the server beyond what is standard in HTTP server logs. Render.com's privacy policy applies: render.com/privacy.

3. Cookies

This Site does not set any first-party cookies. Third-party CDN providers listed above may set cookies in accordance with their own policies. See the Cookie Policy for details.

4. Analytics and tracking

This Site does not use any analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting scripts, or advertising networks. No behavioural data is collected.

5. Legal basis for processing (GDPR — EU/UK users)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom, the legal basis for the third-party CDN connections described in Section 2c is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — specifically, the technically necessary delivery of the application assets. No personal data is processed for any other purpose. No consent banner is required because no non-essential cookies are set and no personal data is collected by the Site operator.

6. CCPA/CPRA — California residents

The Site does not sell, share, or disclose personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. No personal information is collected by the Site operator. California residents have no opt-out action available because there is nothing to opt out of.

7. DPDP Act 2023 — Indian residents

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), the Site operator is a Data Fiduciary only to the limited extent of the CDN connections described in Section 2c. No personal data is processed, stored, or transferred by the Site operator. The third-party CDNs listed above act as independent Data Fiduciaries under their own privacy policies.

8. Data retention

The Site operator retains no personal data. localStorage data (gesture templates) is retained on your device indefinitely until you clear it. Server logs on Render.com are retained according to Render.com's own data retention policy.

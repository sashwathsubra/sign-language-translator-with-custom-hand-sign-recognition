Cookie Policy

Sign Language Translator · Owner: Sashwath Subramanian · Last updated: 14 September 2026

✓ This Site does not set any first-party cookies. No cookie consent banner is shown because none is required.

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are — and are not — used on the Sign Language Translator browser demo.

1. First-party cookies

None. The Site operator does not set any cookies on your device. No session cookies, tracking cookies, preference cookies, or analytics cookies are set by this Site.

2. Browser localStorage

The Site uses your browser's localStorage (under the key slt_gestures) to store gesture template data that you record. This is not a cookie: it is local storage that lives only on your device, is not sent to any server, and is not accessible to any third party. It does not require consent under GDPR or CCPA because it is used solely to provide functionality you explicitly requested (saving your gesture vocabulary).

To clear this data: open your browser's developer tools → Application → Local Storage → select this site → delete the slt_gestures key, or clear all site data.

4. Why there is no cookie consent banner

Under the EU ePrivacy Directive (implemented via national cookie laws) and GDPR, consent banners are required for non-essential cookies — for example, analytics, advertising, or preference cookies. Because:

  • No first-party cookies are set by this Site
  • No analytics or advertising services are used
  • localStorage use is essential to the requested functionality (saving your gesture vocabulary)
  • Third-party CDN connections are technically necessary and constitute legitimate interest

…a consent banner is not legally required and has not been added.

5. How to manage cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of third-party CDN services. Clearing localStorage will delete any gesture templates you have recorded.
